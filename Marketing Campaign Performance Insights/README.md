# 📊 Marketing Campaign Performance Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores marketing campaign data to evaluate campaign
effectiveness, understand customer engagement, and compare performance
across campaign types, marketing channels, companies, customer segments,
languages, time periods, and locations.

The analysis focuses on key performance indicators such as **Conversion
Rate, Acquisition Cost, ROI, Engagement Score, Clicks, and
Impressions**. The goal is to identify patterns that can support better
campaign evaluation and more informed marketing decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

-   Evaluate and compare campaign performance using conversion rate,
    acquisition cost, and ROI.
-   Explore how campaign type and marketing channel relate to campaign
    effectiveness.
-   Understand engagement patterns across campaign types and channels.
-   Compare performance across companies, customer segments, target
    audiences, and languages.
-   Examine changes in conversion rate and engagement over time.
-   Explore geographical differences in acquisition cost and ROI.

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses the **Marketing Campaign** CSV dataset.

**Dataset source:**\
[Download the marketing campaign
dataset](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ArchanaInsights/Datasets/main/marketing_campaign.csv)

### Dataset Description

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Column                              Description
  ----------------------------------- -----------------------------------
  `Campaign_ID`                       Unique identifier for each campaign

  `Company`                           Organization running the campaign

  `Campaign_Type`                     Type of marketing effort, such as
                                      email, social media, influencer,
                                      display, or search

  `Target_Audience`                   Demographic or audience group
                                      targeted by the campaign

  `Duration`                          Campaign duration in days

  `Channels_Used`                     Platforms or mediums used to
                                      promote the campaign

  `Conversion_Rate`                   Percentage of impressions or leads
                                      resulting in desired actions

  `Acquisition_Cost`                  Expense incurred to acquire each
                                      customer

  `ROI`                               Return on investment and an
                                      indicator of campaign profitability

  `Location`                          Geographical area where the
                                      campaign was executed

  `Language`                          Language used for the campaign
                                      content

  `Clicks`                            Total clicks generated by the
                                      campaign

  `Impressions`                       Total times the campaign was
                                      displayed

  `Engagement_Score`                  Engagement score on a scale from 1
                                      to 10

  `Customer_Segment`                  Customer group targeted by the
                                      campaign

  `Date`                              Date on which the campaign occurred
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🔍 Analysis Workflow

### 1. Data Loading

-   Load the CSV dataset into a Pandas DataFrame.

### 2. Descriptive Analysis

-   Inspect the first rows and dataset dimensions.
-   Review column data types and non-null values.
-   Generate descriptive statistics for numerical columns.
-   Check unique campaign identifiers, locations, and customer segments.
-   Review category counts for campaign types and channels.

### 3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

#### 📈 Campaign Performance

-   Examine the relationship between acquisition cost and ROI.
-   Compare average conversion rates by channel and campaign type.
-   Explore engagement score distributions across campaign types.
-   Compare average ROI across companies.
-   Examine the relationship between engagement score and conversion
    rate.

#### 👥 Customer Segmentation

-   Review the distribution of target audiences.
-   Compare conversion rates across customer segments and languages.
-   Explore acquisition cost by customer segment and channel.
-   Compare average conversion rates across languages.

#### 📣 Channel Effectiveness

-   Compare engagement scores across channels and campaign types.
-   Examine the distribution of total ROI across channels.
-   Explore the relationship between clicks and impressions for each
    channel.

#### 📅 Time-Based Analysis

-   Examine campaign duration distribution.
-   Track conversion-rate trends over time for each company.
-   Explore engagement-score trends over time.

#### 🌍 Geographic Analysis

-   Identify locations with the highest acquisition cost.
-   Compare conversion rates across locations and target audiences.
-   Examine the proportion of ROI across locations.
